# ── Cell and membrane physiology ─────────────────────────────────────────

G|Action potential|physiology,neuro|Repolarization of a neuron is caused by ___ efflux|potassium|potassium,k,k+|After sodium channels inactivate.

G|Neuromuscular junction|physiology,neuro,NMJ|Acetylcholine release at the nerve terminal requires ___ influx|calcium|calcium,ca,ca2+|Blocked in Lambert-Eaton syndrome.

# ── Cardiovascular physiology ────────────────────────────────────────────
G|Baroreceptors|physiology,cardio|Carotid sinus baroreceptors signal via cranial nerve ___|IX|ix,9,nine,glossopharyngeal|Aortic arch receptors use the vagus.
B|Baroreceptor reflex|physiology,cardio|A fall in blood pressure causes baroreceptor firing to|Decrease|Increase|Less inhibition means more sympathetic outflow.
G|Starling forces|physiology,cardio|Edema forms when capillary ___ pressure exceeds oncotic pressure|hydrostatic|hydrostatic|Or when oncotic pressure falls, as in nephrotic syndrome.
B|Vascular resistance|physiology,cardio|Resistance is most sensitive to changes in|Vessel radius|Vessel length|Resistance varies with the fourth power of radius.
G|Coronary flow|physiology,cardio|Coronary flow is driven by ___ pressure during diastole|aortic diastolic|aortic diastolic,diastolic,aortic|Which is why tachycardia worsens ischemia.
B|Cardiac oxygen demand|physiology,cardio|Myocardial oxygen demand rises most with|Heart rate and wall stress|Preload and nothing else|Rate-pressure product estimates it.

# ── Respiratory physiology ───────────────────────────────────────────────

G|Pulmonary circulation|physiology,resp|Alveolar hypoxia causes pulmonary vessels to ___|constrict|constrict,vasoconstrict|The opposite of systemic vessels.
B|Zones of the lung|physiology,resp|In lung zone 1, ventilation exceeds perfusion because|Alveolar pressure exceeds arterial pressure|Gravity increases flow at the apex|Zone 1 expands in hypovolemia.
