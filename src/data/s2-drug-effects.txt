# Drug side effects and interactions — gap-weighted, one drug several angles.

# ── Signature toxicities ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
G|Amiodarone|pharm,resp,toxicity|Amiodarone can cause pulmonary ___ requiring baseline lung function tests|fibrosis|fibrosis|Plus thyroid, hepatic and corneal deposits.
G|Amiodarone|pharm,eye,toxicity|Amiodarone deposits in the ___, causing halos|cornea|cornea,corneal|Usually reversible and rarely limits therapy.
G|Bleomycin|pharm,oncology,resp|Bleomycin causes pulmonary ___, worsened by high oxygen|fibrosis|fibrosis|Which matters during anaesthesia.
G|Cyclophosphamide|pharm,oncology,renal|Cyclophosphamide causes haemorrhagic ___ from acrolein|cystitis|cystitis|Prevented with mesna and hydration.
G|Cisplatin|pharm,oncology,electrolytes|Cisplatin classically wastes ___ in the urine|magnesium|magnesium,mg|Along with nephrotoxicity and ototoxicity.
G|Vincristine|pharm,oncology,neuro|Vincristine causes peripheral ___ rather than marrow suppression|neuropathy|neuropathy|Vinblastine blasts the bone marrow instead.
G|Doxorubicin|pharm,oncology,cardio|Doxorubicin causes dilated ___ in a dose-dependent way|cardiomyopathy|cardiomyopathy|Monitor ejection fraction; dexrazoxane protects.
G|Methotrexate|pharm,rheum,liver|Long-term methotrexate requires monitoring of the ___ and blood count|liver|liver,lfts,liver function|Plus folic acid supplementation.
G|Tamoxifen|pharm,oncology,repro|Tamoxifen raises the risk of ___ carcinoma and thromboembolism|endometrial|endometrial|An estrogen agonist in the uterus.
G|Trastuzumab|pharm,oncology,cardio|Trastuzumab cardiotoxicity is usually ___, unlike anthracyclines|reversible|reversible|Monitor ejection fraction during treatment.

# ── Endocrine and metabolic effects ──────────────────────────────────────
G|Thiazides|pharm,endocrine,electrolytes|Thiazides raise calcium, glucose, urate and ___|lipids|lipids,cholesterol,triglycerides|And lower sodium, potassium and magnesium.
G|Corticosteroids|pharm,endocrine|Chronic steroids cause ___, osteoporosis and immunosuppression|hyperglycemia|hyperglycemia,diabetes,high blood sugar|Plus adrenal suppression on withdrawal.
G|Atypical antipsychotics|pharm,psych,endocrine|Olanzapine and clozapine most cause weight gain and ___|diabetes|diabetes,hyperglycemia,metabolic syndrome|Monitor weight, lipids and glucose.
G|Protease inhibitors|pharm,HIV,endocrine|Protease inhibitors cause ___ and insulin resistance|lipodystrophy|lipodystrophy|Fat redistribution with hyperlipidemia.
G|Thionamides|pharm,endocrine,heme|Propylthiouracil carries a black box warning for ___ failure|liver|liver,hepatic|Methimazole is preferred outside the first trimester.
B|Lithium|pharm,psych,endocrine|Lithium causes|Hypothyroidism and hypercalcemia|Hyperthyroidism and hypocalcemia|Plus nephrogenic diabetes insipidus.
G|Phenytoin|pharm,neuro,derm|Phenytoin classically causes ___ hyperplasia and hirsutism|gingival|gingival,gum,gums|Plus ataxia, nystagmus and megaloblastic anemia.

# ── Haematological effects ───────────────────────────────────────────────
G|Chloramphenicol|pharm,micro,heme|Chloramphenicol can cause irreversible ___ anemia|aplastic|aplastic|Plus dose-dependent marrow suppression and grey baby syndrome.
G|Clozapine|pharm,psych,heme|Clozapine can cause ___, requiring regular neutrophil counts|agranulocytosis|agranulocytosis|Roughly 1 percent risk.
G|Heparin|pharm,coagulation,heme|Antibodies against platelet factor 4 bound to ___ cause HIT|heparin|heparin|Platelets fall after 5 to 10 days, with thrombosis not bleeding.
G|Methyldopa|pharm,cardio,heme|Methyldopa causes a positive direct ___ test|Coombs|coombs,antiglobulin|Usually without clinical hemolysis.
G|Dapsone|pharm,micro,heme|Dapsone causes ___ and hemolysis in G6PD deficiency|methemoglobinemia|methemoglobinemia,methaemoglobinaemia|Chocolate-brown blood, treated with methylene blue.
B|Linezolid|pharm,micro,heme|Prolonged linezolid causes|Thrombocytopenia and optic neuropathy|Nephrotoxicity and electrolyte loss|Limit courses beyond two weeks.

# ── Renal and electrolyte effects ────────────────────────────────────────
G|Aminoglycosides|pharm,micro,renal|Aminoglycosides accumulate in the proximal ___, causing non-oliguric failure|tubule|tubule,tubules,tubular cells|Monitor levels and renal function.
G|NSAIDs|pharm,renal|NSAIDs cause acute kidney injury by removing ___ vasodilation|prostaglandin|prostaglandin,prostaglandins|Dangerous with volume depletion, ACE inhibitors and diuretics.

G|Amphotericin B|pharm,fungi,electrolytes|Amphotericin wastes potassium and ___ in the urine|magnesium|magnesium,mg|Plus renal tubular acidosis.
G|Cyclosporine|pharm,transplant,renal|Cyclosporine causes dose-dependent ___ and hypertension|nephrotoxicity|nephrotoxicity,renal impairment|Plus gum hypertrophy and hirsutism.
B|Tenofovir|pharm,HIV,renal|Tenofovir disoproxil can cause|Fanconi syndrome and bone loss|Hepatic steatosis and lactate|The alafenamide form is safer.

G|Statins|pharm,lipids,liver|Statins can raise ___, though clinically significant injury is rare|transaminases|transaminases,alt,liver enzymes|Check a baseline before starting.

G|Anticholinergic burden|pharm,geriatrics,psych|A high anticholinergic burden in the elderly worsens ___|confusion|confusion,delirium,cognition|Diphenhydramine and oxybutynin are common culprits.
