# ── Michaelis-Menten ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
G|Michaelis-Menten kinetics|pharm,principles,biochem|___ is the substrate concentration at half of maximum velocity|Km|km|A measure of affinity: the enzyme's working range.
B|Michaelis-Menten kinetics|pharm,principles,biochem|A HIGHER Km means affinity is|Lower|Higher|More substrate is needed to reach half-maximal rate.
G|Michaelis-Menten kinetics|pharm,principles|Vmax is directly proportional to the ___ concentration|enzyme|enzyme,enzymes|More enzyme, higher ceiling. Km is unchanged.
BT|Enzyme inhibitors|pharm,principles,biochem|On a Lineweaver-Burk plot, which inhibitor lines CROSS?|Competitive|Noncompetitive|Competitive inhibitors share a Vmax, so the lines meet on the y-axis.
B|Competitive inhibitor|pharm,principles|A competitive inhibitor changes|Km only|Vmax only|Raises Km, Vmax unchanged. Overcome by more substrate.
B|Noncompetitive inhibitor|pharm,principles|A noncompetitive inhibitor changes|Vmax only|Km only|Lowers Vmax, Km unchanged. Cannot be outcompeted.
