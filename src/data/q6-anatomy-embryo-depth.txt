# ── Biostatistics: study design ──────────────────────────────────────────
G|Randomization|biostats|Randomization controls for ___ confounders, both known and unknown|all|all,unknown,both|Its unique advantage over observational designs.
B|Blinding|biostats|Double blinding means|Neither participant nor investigator knows|Only the participant is kept unaware|It reduces observer and reporting bias.
G|Meta-analysis|biostats|A meta-analysis pools results to increase statistical ___|power|power|Vulnerable to publication bias.
B|Funnel plot|biostats|An asymmetric funnel plot suggests|Publication bias|Effect modification|Small negative studies are missing.
G|Number needed to harm|biostats|Number needed to harm is 1 divided by the attributable ___|risk|risk,risk increase|Higher is safer.
B|Non-inferiority trial|biostats|A non-inferiority trial aims to show the new treatment is|Not meaningfully worse|Clearly better|Used when the new option has other advantages.
G|Intention to treat|biostats|Intention-to-treat analysis preserves the benefit of ___|randomization|randomization,randomisation|Per-protocol analysis breaks it.
B|Case series|biostats,epidemiology|A case series|Cannot establish causation|Establishes causation well|No comparison group.

# ── Biostatistics: measures ──────────────────────────────────────────────

G|Odds ratio|biostats,epidemiology|The odds ratio approximates relative risk when the disease is ___|rare|rare,uncommon|The rare disease assumption.
B|Attributable risk|biostats,epidemiology|Attributable risk is|Risk in exposed minus risk in unexposed|The ratio of exposed to unexposed risk|The ratio is relative risk.

B|Likelihood ratio|biostats,lab|A positive likelihood ratio above 10|Strongly rules in disease|Rules out disease|Below 0.1 strongly rules it out.
