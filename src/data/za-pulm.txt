B|Type II pneumocyte role|resp,path|Type II pneumocytes act as|The stem cells of the alveolus|The main gas-exchanging cell|They repopulate damaged type I cells.

B|Adenocarcinoma population|resp,oncology|Lung adenocarcinoma is the answer for cancer in|A non-smoker|A heavy smoker|Classically a woman who never smoked.
B|Radon and lung cancer|resp,oncology,clinical|Lung cancer in a non-smoker can be attributed to|Background radon exposure|Passive alcohol exposure|An accepted environmental cause.
B|Bronchial carcinoid secretion|resp,oncology,endocrine|A bronchial carcinoid tumor secretes|Serotonin|Insulin|Causing flushing and diarrhea.
B|Mesothelioma cell of origin|resp,oncology,path|Mesothelioma arises from|Mesothelial cells|Alveolar epithelial cells|After asbestos exposure.
B|Asbestos sequence|resp,oncology,path|After asbestos exposure|Asbestosis comes first, then mesothelioma|Mesothelioma comes first, then fibrosis|Separated by many years.
