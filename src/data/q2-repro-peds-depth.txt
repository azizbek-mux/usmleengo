# ── Menstrual cycle ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
G|Menstrual cycle|repro,endocrine|Ovulation is triggered by a surge of ___|LH|lh,luteinizing hormone|About 36 hours before the egg is released.
B|Luteal phase|repro,endocrine|The luteal phase is|Consistently 14 days|Highly variable|The follicular phase varies instead.
G|Corpus luteum|repro,endocrine|The corpus luteum secretes ___ to maintain the endometrium|progesterone|progesterone|Rescued by hCG if pregnancy occurs.
B|Estrogen|repro,endocrine|Estrogen exerts positive feedback on LH|Just before ovulation|Throughout the cycle|Otherwise it is inhibitory.
G|Inhibin|repro,endocrine|Inhibin selectively suppresses ___|FSH|fsh|From granulosa cells; falls in menopause.

# ── Pregnancy physiology ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
G|hCG|repro,OB,endocrine|hCG is detectable in serum about ___ days after conception|8|8,eight,8-11|It doubles roughly every 48 hours early on.
B|hCG|repro,OB,oncology|An hCG far higher than expected for dates suggests|A molar pregnancy or twins|Ectopic pregnancy|Ectopic hCG rises abnormally slowly instead.
G|Pregnancy cardiovascular|repro,OB,cardio|Plasma volume rises about ___ percent in pregnancy|50|50,fifty,40-50|Causing a dilutional physiologic anemia.
B|Pregnancy respiratory|repro,OB,resp|In pregnancy, tidal volume|Rises|Falls|Residual volume falls; a compensated respiratory alkalosis results.
G|Placenta|repro,OB,physiology|The fetal side of the placenta is derived from the ___|chorion|chorion,trophoblast,chorion frondosum|The maternal side is the decidua basalis.

# ── Obstetric complications ──────────────────────────────────────────────

B|Ectopic pregnancy|repro,OB,pharm|Methotrexate for ectopic pregnancy requires|An unruptured small ectopic with stable vitals|Any ectopic once the diagnosis is made|Rupture demands surgery.
G|Gestational diabetes|repro,OB,endocrine|Gestational diabetes is screened at ___ weeks|24|24,24-28,28|With a glucose challenge test.
B|Gestational diabetes|repro,OB,peds|The neonate of a diabetic mother is at risk of|Hypoglycemia after birth|Hyperglycemia at birth|Fetal hyperinsulinemia persists once the glucose supply stops.
G|Preeclampsia|repro,OB,pharm|Low-dose ___ from the second trimester reduces preeclampsia risk|aspirin|aspirin|Offered to high-risk women from 12 weeks.
B|HELLP syndrome|repro,OB,heme|HELLP syndrome consists of hemolysis, elevated liver enzymes and|Low platelets|Low potassium|A severe variant of preeclampsia.
G|Magnesium toxicity|repro,OB,pharm|The first sign of magnesium toxicity is loss of deep tendon ___|reflexes|reflexes,reflex|Then respiratory depression. Reverse with calcium gluconate.
B|Rh isoimmunization|repro,OB,immuno|Anti-D immunoglobulin is given to|Rh-negative mothers|Rh-positive mothers|At 28 weeks and after delivery or any sensitizing event.
G|Postpartum hemorrhage|repro,OB,emergency|The commonest cause of postpartum hemorrhage is uterine ___|atony|atony,atonia|Massage, oxytocin, then further uterotonics.
B|Amniotic fluid|repro,OB,peds|Meconium-stained fluid raises the risk of|Meconium aspiration syndrome|Neural tube defects|Especially in post-term deliveries.
G|Preterm labour|repro,OB,pharm|Antenatal ___ are given before 34 weeks to mature fetal lungs|corticosteroids|corticosteroids,steroids,betamethasone|Reduces respiratory distress and intraventricular hemorrhage.

B|Menopause|repro,gyn,endocrine|Menopause is diagnosed after|12 months of amenorrhea|6 months of amenorrhea|Confirmed clinically; FSH is not usually needed.
