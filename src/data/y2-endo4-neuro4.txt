# Endocrine fourth pass and neurology fourth pass. Gap-weighted.

# ── Endocrine physiology applied ─────────────────────────────────────────
G|Hormone feedback|endocrine,physiology|Most endocrine axes are controlled by ___ feedback|negative|negative|Positive feedback is rare, as in the LH surge.
B|Free versus total hormone|endocrine,lab|The biologically active fraction of a hormone is|The free fraction|The protein-bound fraction|Which is why binding protein changes mislead.
G|Pulsatility|endocrine,physiology|___ secretion of GnRH is required for gonadotropin release|Pulsatile|pulsatile,pulsed|Continuous exposure suppresses the axis.
B|Diurnal variation|endocrine,lab|Which hormone must be timed to the morning?|Cortisol|Prolactin|Testosterone is also best measured early.
G|Stimulation versus suppression|endocrine,lab|___ tests are used when hormone excess is suspected|Suppression|suppression|Stimulation tests are used for suspected deficiency.
B|Insulin tolerance test|endocrine,lab|The insulin tolerance test assesses|Growth hormone and cortisol reserve|Thyroid and parathyroid function|Contraindicated in epilepsy and cardiac disease.

# ── Diabetes complications ───────────────────────────────────────────────

G|Diabetic retinopathy screening|endocrine,eye|Diabetic retinal screening should occur at least ___|annually|annually,yearly,every year|From diagnosis in type 2, from puberty in type 1.

B|Hypoglycemia unawareness|endocrine,diabetes|Hypoglycemia unawareness can be partly reversed by|Avoiding hypoglycemia for several weeks|Tighter glucose control for weeks|Raising the threshold for symptoms again.

# ── Thyroid and parathyroid applied ──────────────────────────────────────
