# ── Antibiotic selection by syndrome ─────────────────────────────────────
G|Community pneumonia|micro,resp,pharm|Mild community-acquired pneumonia is treated with ___|amoxicillin|amoxicillin,amoxycillin,doxycycline|Adding a macrolide if atypical cover is needed.
B|Hospital pneumonia|micro,resp,pharm|Hospital-acquired pneumonia needs cover for|Pseudomonas and resistant gram negatives|Atypical organisms and anaerobes|Piperacillin-tazobactam or a carbapenem.
G|Cellulitis|micro,derm,pharm|Non-purulent cellulitis is treated with a ___-active antibiotic|streptococcal|streptococcal,strep|Flucloxacillin or cefalexin.
B|Diabetic foot infection|micro,endocrine,pharm|Moderate diabetic foot infection needs cover for|Gram positives, negatives and anaerobes|Gram positive skin organisms only|Often polymicrobial.
G|Meningitis empirical|micro,neuro,pharm|Empirical meningitis therapy in adults is ___ plus vancomycin|ceftriaxone|ceftriaxone,cefotaxime|Adding ampicillin for Listeria in the elderly.
B|Endocarditis empirical|micro,cardio,pharm|Empirical native valve endocarditis therapy covers|Staphylococci, streptococci and enterococci|Gram negatives and anaerobic organisms|Usually vancomycin with gentamicin pending cultures.
G|Intra-abdominal sepsis|micro,GI,pharm|Intra-abdominal sepsis requires ___ cover alongside gram negatives|anaerobic|anaerobic,anaerobe|Metronidazole or a beta-lactam-inhibitor combination.
B|Urinary sepsis|micro,renal,pharm|Urosepsis is treated empirically with|A broad-spectrum beta-lactam|Oral nitrofurantoin alone|Nitrofurantoin does not reach tissue levels.
G|Septic arthritis|micro,msk,pharm|Septic arthritis is treated with ___ plus joint drainage|flucloxacillin|flucloxacillin,vancomycin,antibiotics|Guided by Gram stain and local resistance.
B|Neutropenic sepsis|micro,oncology,pharm|Empirical therapy in neutropenic sepsis must cover|Pseudomonas|Anaerobes only|Piperacillin-tazobactam or meropenem.
