B|Phase I trial|biostats,pharm|A phase I trial primarily assesses|Safety in healthy volunteers|Efficacy in patients|Small numbers; dose-finding and toxicity.
B|Phase II trial|biostats,pharm|A phase II trial primarily assesses|Efficacy in patients with the disease|Safety after marketing approval|Moderate size; does it work?
B|Phase III trial|biostats,pharm|A phase III trial is|A large randomized controlled trial|An uncontrolled surveillance survey|Compares against standard treatment before approval.
B|Phase IV trial|biostats,pharm|A phase IV study looks for|Rare adverse events after marketing|Initial dose-finding and safety|Post-marketing surveillance; can trigger withdrawal.

B|Prevalence|biostats,epidemiology|Better treatment that prolongs survival will|Increase prevalence|Decrease prevalence|Prevalence equals incidence times duration.

B|Specificity|biostats|A highly specific test is best used to|Rule in disease|Rule out disease|SpPin: a positive result with a specific test rules in.

B|Cross-sectional study|biostats,epidemiology|A cross-sectional study measures|Prevalence at a single point in time|Incidence accumulating over time|A snapshot; cannot establish causality.

B|Control selection|biostats,epidemiology|Controls in a case-control study should be|People without the disease regardless of exposure|People without the exposure being studied|Selecting on exposure creates bias.

B|Length-time bias|biostats,epidemiology|Length-time bias occurs because screening preferentially detects|Slowly progressive disease|Aggressive disease|Indolent cases spend longer in the detectable phase.
B|Recall bias|biostats,epidemiology|Recall bias is most problematic in|Case-control studies|Randomized trials|Cases remember exposures more thoroughly than controls.
B|Hawthorne effect|biostats,epidemiology|The Hawthorne effect is when subjects|Change behavior because they are observed|Recall past exposures inaccurately|A form of observation bias.
B|Intention to treat|biostats|Intention-to-treat analysis includes patients|In their originally assigned groups|Only if they completed treatment|Preserves randomization and avoids bias.
B|Incidence versus prevalence|biostats,epidemiology|Incidence measures|New cases over a period|All existing cases|Prevalence includes both new and existing cases.
