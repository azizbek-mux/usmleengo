# ── Cardiac examination and signs ────────────────────────────────────────
G|Apex beat|cardio,exam|A displaced, diffuse apex beat suggests ___ overload|volume|volume|A heaving apex suggests pressure overload.
B|Thrill|cardio,exam|A palpable thrill always indicates|At least a grade 4 murmur|A benign flow murmur|Grading runs from 1 to 6.
G|Third heart sound|cardio,exam|An S3 is normal in young adults and in ___|pregnancy|pregnancy|Pathological after about 40.
B|Fourth heart sound|cardio,exam|An S4 is never heard in|Atrial fibrillation|Hypertension|It requires atrial contraction.
G|Splitting of S2|cardio,exam|Wide FIXED splitting of the second heart sound indicates an ___ septal defect|atrial|atrial,asd|Fixed because right-sided filling is constant.
B|Paradoxical splitting|cardio,exam|Paradoxical splitting of S2 suggests|Left bundle branch block or aortic stenosis|Right bundle branch block or stenosis|Aortic closure is delayed past pulmonary.
G|Pulsus alternans|cardio,exam,heart failure|Pulsus ___ is alternating strong and weak beats in severe left ventricular failure|alternans|alternans|A poor prognostic sign.
B|Corrigan pulse|cardio,exam,valve|A collapsing water-hammer pulse indicates|Aortic regurgitation|Aortic stenosis|Wide pulse pressure.
G|Hepatojugular reflux|cardio,exam|A positive hepatojugular reflux suggests right ___ failure|heart|heart,ventricular,ventricle|Sustained JVP elevation on abdominal pressure.
